## B1 — periodic panel slowdown

**Owner report:** A mature factory dropped from roughly 55 FPS to 22 FPS every
15 seconds for nearly two seconds. ExpertMode was false, the panel was visible,
and WHITE was selected. The first correction, installed as `2.2.100.ff71d06`,
did not improve it. Hiding the overlay stopped the dip. A fresh comparison with
public `2.1.83.22a5998` also showed a smaller, approximately 19 FPS drop only
while its panel was visible. This superseded the initial report that the public
version had no dip. At that point this blocked the playthrough.

**First correction (failed runtime retest):** Reused each native station's slot ownership and native research
prerequisites, observed receipts only in selected ILS Automation, omitted the unused
full-state diagnostic export during panel analysis, and reused already-read
availability fields. No new measurement, telemetry cadence or diagnostic system.
The [execution record](GUIDE3-EXECUTION.md#gc3-13-b1--periodic-panel-slowdown)
contains the reasons and technical checks.

**Follow-up:** The owner included the inherited roughly 19 FPS hit in this build.
The follow-up stopped building unused player/factory diagnostics, storage-container details,
entity counts and station-stock aggregates for panel inputs. It used native stock
counts for WHITE/Antimatter, native research flags and native Dyson power.
Construction/launcher details stayed in deliberate exports; recipe reads were
bounded to native cursors and omitted assemblers outside DYSON. Both published and
candidate binaries performed the discarded work; static comparison did not
establish how much of either slowdown it caused. No new instrumentation or
diagnostic export was part of this correction.

**Owner disposition (2026-09-16):** The remaining hit was roughly 7 FPS and was
acceptable. B1 was closed by that explicit owner acceptance. The ExpertMode flag
worked; the owner requested WHITE as its effective phase so every Cube counter
would be displayed.
The owner chose the accepted candidate for the full playthrough regardless
of that fix. GC3-13/G5 stayed open until the 2026-09-19 disposition below.
